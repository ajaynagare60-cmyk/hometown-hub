\# Hometown Hub



\## Project Description



Hometown Hub is a digital community platform designed to connect people who share the same hometown, village, or city.



The platform allows users to communicate with their hometown communities, share posts, discover local events, receive notifications, and participate in community activities.



The project provides a structured alternative to unorganized communication through multiple messaging groups.



\---



\## Problem Statement



People who move away from their hometown often find it difficult to stay connected with their local community.



Information about events, announcements, local news, cultural programs, and community activities is often distributed through different messaging groups and can become difficult to find.



Hometown Hub provides a centralized platform where community members can access and share relevant information.



\---



\## Objectives



\- Connect people with their hometown communities.

\- Provide a centralized platform for community communication.

\- Allow users to create and share posts.

\- Allow users to like and comment on posts.

\- Provide community event management.

\- Allow users to join events and view participants.

\- Provide notifications for important community activities.

\- Provide administrative management features.

\- Provide moderator functionality for community management.

\- Provide Pandit registration and approval functionality.
